CRICBIDS GUIDE

How to run a successful cricket auction

Follow these steps to set up, conduct, and share your auction.
1

Sign up or sign in

Create an account with email and password, or continue with Google. Sign in to access your auction workspace.

2

Create your auction

Open Auction → Auction Management. Enter the tournament name, date, logo, player and team limits, starting bid, and bid increment.

3

Add teams

Open Teams → Add Team and create each team with its name, logo, owner details, and available points or budget.

4

Add player categories and players

Use Players → Player Categories for different price groups. Then use Players → Add Player, share the player registration link, or use Player List to bulk upload players.

5

Conduct live bidding

Open Auction → Live Auction. Cricbids selects available players, lets you record bids, and updates the sold player and team balance automatically.

6

Share results and track progress

Use Auction → Reports for auction summaries. Share the live watch link so team owners and viewers can follow purchases as they happen.
